# Revenant Contributor Licence Agreement

Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor Licence
Agreement v2.2, which is the most widely read agreement of this kind. The
substance is unchanged except that the grant runs to Locke Werks rather than
to a foundation. It is reproduced here rather than linked so that what you
agreed to is in the repository at the commit you agreed to it.

## Why this exists

Revenant is GPL-3.0-or-later. That is not in question and this agreement does
not change it: every contribution is published under the project's licence,
and anyone who receives the software receives it under the GPL.

What the agreement preserves is the ability to change the licence later. Today
Locke Werks holds the copyright on every line, so the project can be
relicensed at any time. The first contribution accepted without an agreement
ends that permanently, because relicensing would then need the permission of
everyone who ever contributed, and in practice that permission is impossible
to collect. Signing this keeps a door open. It does not oblige anyone to walk
through it, and it does not take your copyright away: you keep it, and you can
do whatever else you like with your own work.

If you would rather not sign, say so on the issue or pull request. Small fixes
can usually be handled another way, and an idea described in an issue is worth
having whether or not you write the patch.

## How to sign

Comment on your first pull request with exactly this line, filling in your own
details:

```
I have read the Revenant CLA and I agree to it.
Name: <your full name>
GitHub: @<your username>
Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

That comment, on a pull request from your own account, is the record. There is
no bot and no external service: this project does not have the contribution
volume to justify one, and a signature in the thread is easier to audit than a
row in somebody else's database.

Contributing on behalf of a company, rather than as an individual, needs a
corporate agreement instead. Open an issue and it will be sorted out.
